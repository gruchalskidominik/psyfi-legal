# Privacy Policy for PSYFI

Last updated: May 18, 2026

GR Softhouse ("we", "us", or "our") operates the PSYFI mobile application.

## Information We Collect

We collect information you provide: name, email address, and data you enter 
during use of the app (attachment style quiz results, conversation data).

## How We Use Your Information

- To provide and improve our services
- To generate personalized psychological insights
- To send service-related communications

## Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase. We do not sell your personal 
data to third parties.

## AI Processing

Conversations are processed by Anthropic's Claude AI to generate insights. 
Data is handled per Anthropic's privacy policy at anthropic.com/privacy.

## Mental Health Disclaimer

PSYFI is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. It is an 
educational and self-development tool. If you are in crisis, please contact 
emergency services (112) or a mental health helpline.
